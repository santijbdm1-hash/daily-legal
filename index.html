Legal & privacy

My Journal · local-only journal · effective 28 October 2026

My Journal is a private daily journal that runs entirely on your device. There is no account, no cloud sync, no analytics and no ads. The documents below explain your rights, how data is handled, and the terms that apply to the app.

Documents

  • Privacy Policy — what is stored, where it stays, and how to delete it.
  • Terms of Use / EULA — the licence and rules for using the app.
  • Delete your data — step-by-step removal instructions.
  • Legal Notice — publisher, consumer and intellectual-property information.
  • Licenses & notices — open-source software and font licences.

At a glance

  • Journal data stays in the app’s local storage on your device.
  • No account, no server-side journal, no analytics, no ads.
  • The only copy that leaves the device is a backup you choose to export.
  • Android backup is disabled, so the journal is not copied to Google’s cloud backup.
  • You can delete everything from Account → Delete all data.
